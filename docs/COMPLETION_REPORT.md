## Design decisions and honest limits

* **No Unity WebGL.** The WebGL module is not installed, and AR Foundation does not give camera AR in WebGL anyway. The QR therefore opens a React/three.js site (real model, real picking) and uses `<model-viewer>` for install-free AR. Unity is the native/desktop app and an optional Android ARCore build.
* **Model fidelity.** Procedural primitives: recognisable but schematic. Receiver/drier, sight glass, service port and gauges are marked illustrative; gauges show tick marks only, no numbers.
* **Flow animation** is conceptual (colour = general refrigerant state) and is labelled as not CFD / not a thermodynamic solver. The p–h figure is a qualitative sketch labelled "schematic · not to scale · no measured data".
